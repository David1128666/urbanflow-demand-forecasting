# Code of Conduct

> **中文说明：** 参与者应保持尊重、建设性和专业沟通，尊重数据隐私和知识
> 产权。违反规范的行为可能导致评论被删除、Issue 被关闭或贡献被拒绝。

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback gracefully.
- Focus discussions on the project and reproducible evidence.
- Respect data licenses, privacy, and intellectual property.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwanted attention.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior that violates this
code of conduct.

## Reporting

Security issues should follow `SECURITY.md`. For other conduct concerns,
contact the maintainers through the contact method published on the GitHub
repository.
